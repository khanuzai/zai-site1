## what it is

a real-scale model of the solar system with every planet on its orbit, and every planet clickable.

## why i built it

i'm a big stargazer. space, the milky way, all of it. i thought it was the coolest thing ever, and i still do, so i built one.

## how it works

built with three.js and webgl, running entirely in the browser.
